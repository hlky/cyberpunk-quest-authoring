# Braindance and specialized scenes

This section is experimental until a complete custom candidate has passed
runtime acceptance.

Planned research subjects:

- the role of `.scenerid`;
- actor, body, facial, cyberware, and camera channels;
- the RID-to-scene-to-quest resource chain;
- recorded-perspective cameras;
- rewindable sections and playback events;
- visual, audio, and thermal clue layers;
- visibility, exit, interruption, and cleanup structures;
- forward seek, rewind, normal cleanup, interrupted cleanup, and replay.

Conceptual documentation may be published before the end-to-end authoring
workflow is declared runtime-proven.
