# Communities and characters

This section will explain the complete actor lifecycle:

```text
activate community
  -> wait for the required actor to spawn
  -> begin gameplay or a scene
  -> consume the outcome
  -> clean up at a safe boundary
```

Planned subjects:

- compiled community areas and community registries;
- entries, phases, AI spots, and workspots;
- activation, acquisition, spawn readiness, and deactivation;
- gameplay AI tiers and follower roles;
- hostility and combat threats;
- actor persistence across child phases;
- `.ent`, `.app`, TweakDB character records, and appearance namespaces;
- custom character authoring as a later advanced track.
