# Troubleshooting

Successful serialization and packing do not prove that a quest is valid at
runtime.

This section will cover:

- malformed or forward handle references;
- missing registrations and incorrect depot paths;
- NodeRef and phase-prefab resolution;
- actor/community readiness;
- save-backed facts, journal entries, scenes, and device state;
- journal path and localization lookup failures;
- embedded choice localization ordering;
- lipsync resource cardinality;
- streaming and fast-travel lifecycle failures;
- clean-save testing;
- controlled isolation and evidence recording.

Every failure guide should separate observed evidence from discarded
hypotheses.
