# Reference

Use this section when you know the question but do not need another tutorial.
It maps player-facing concepts to native resources, keeps identifier domains
separate, and points each structural or runtime claim back to its evidence
boundary.

The vanilla index cites depot paths and focused properties; it does not contain
extracted game resources. Legacy Ghostline results remain evidence for their
named historical candidates, commits, and archive hashes. They are never a
reader prerequisite and do not silently promote a new lab under the current
pinned baseline.

For failures rather than lookup, start with [Troubleshooting](../troubleshooting/index.md).
