# Journal, UI, and localization

This section will explain:

- quests, phases, objectives, descriptions, and mappins;
- contacts, conversations, messages, choices, and replies;
- files, emails, readable shards, and onscreens;
- full journal paths and `fileEntryIndex`;
- activation, inactive, succeeded, and failed states;
- map tracking and navigation;
- quest completion and rewards;
- the separate lookup paths for journal/UI text, spoken dialogue, and embedded
  scene choice text.

Similar-looking IDs from these systems are not interchangeable. Lookup chains
will be shown as explicit resource diagrams.
